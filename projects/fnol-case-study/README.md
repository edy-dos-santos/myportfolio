# FNOL Assistant: a conversational AI case study

One scenario, four projects: a car insurance **first-notification-of-loss (FNOL)** assistant that helps a customer report an accident, designed, built, tested and risk-reviewed end to end.

> Speculative portfolio work. The brand ("Wattle Car Insurance"), policies, numbers and service levels are fictional. Not affiliated with any insurer.

| # | Project | What it shows | Status |
|---|---------|---------------|--------|
| 1 | [Design: flows, persona, scripts](01-design/) | Flow design, persona and tone, dialogue writing, journey mapping, specs | Done |
| 2 | Working prototype + conversation logs | Built on Claude; 30-50 test conversations, failure tagging, before/after fixes | Next |
| 3 | Usability and A/B test | Story-first vs form-first capture; completion, time, satisfaction | Planned |
| 4 | Compliance and risk layer | Risk register, controls, escalation triggers, review checklist | Planned |
