# Participant information and consent

**Study:** Story-first vs form-first: how people report a car insurance claim to a chat assistant

**Researcher:** [name and contact, to be added by the study team]

## What this study is about

We are testing two ways a chat assistant can ask for the details of a car insurance claim. The assistant is called Sam. Sam is fictional, and so is the insurer, Wattle Car Insurance. We want to know which way is easier and which way people prefer.

## What you will do

- You will sit with the researcher for about 50 minutes.
- You will chat with Sam twice, on a laptop. Each time you will use a made-up scenario on a card. The card tells you who you are, what happened and what policy you have.
- After each chat you will answer three or four short questions.
- At the end you will tell us which chat you would rather use, and why.

You will not be asked about your own car, your own accident or your own policy.

## Recording

If you agree, we will record the screen and the audio of the session. We use the recording only to check our notes. If you do not agree, we will take written notes only. You can change your mind at any time before the session ends.

## Your information

- We do not ask for your real name in the chat. Use a made-up name on the card.
- We store the notes, the chat transcripts, the questionnaire answers and any recording on a secured drive.
- We remove your name and contact details from the data used in analysis. We use a participant code (for example R01) instead.
- We keep the data for [retention period, to be set by the study team and the ethics approval], then delete it.
- We handle your personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.
- If this study is run through a university, it needs ethics review under the National Statement on Ethical Conduct in Human Research. The approval reference will be given to you on request: [reference, to be added].

## Your choices

- Taking part is voluntary.
- You can stop at any time, for any reason. You keep the gift card if you stop.
- You do not have to answer any question.
- If the session upsets you, we will pause or stop, and offer support. Lifeline is 13 11 14 (24 hours).
