# Screener: who to invite

Use this with each person who answers the call for participants. Ask the questions in order. Stop at the first disqualifier and thank the person.

## Questions

1. Are you 18 or older? *(Qualify: yes. Disqualify: no.)*
2. Do you drive a car, and do you hold car insurance in Australia? *(Qualify: yes to both. Disqualify: no to either.)*
3. What is your age band? 18-34 / 35-54 / 55 or older. *(Record for quota. Any answer qualifies, subject to quotas.)*
4. Do you, or does anyone in your household, work in insurance, claims, or a broker or repairer? *(Disqualify: yes.)*
5. Do you work in user experience (UX) design, usability testing or market research? *(Disqualify: yes.)*
6. How comfortable are you using a website or app chat, such as a bank or retail chat? Not at all / somewhat / very. *(Record for quota: low / medium / high. Any answer qualifies, subject to quotas.)*
7. Have you lodged a car insurance claim before? Yes / no. *(Record for quota: yes counts towards the "lodged a claim before" quota.)*
8. In the last 12 months, were you involved in a car crash where anyone was injured? *(Disqualify: yes. This protects participants from distress.)*
9. Do you have a quiet place, and a laptop or computer you can use for about 50 minutes? *(Disqualify: no.)*
10. Are you OK with the researcher taking written notes during the session? Screen and audio recording is optional and only with your consent. *(Qualify: yes. Disqualify: no.)*
11. Can you attend a session at a set time in the next four weeks? *(Disqualify: no.)*
12. Is there anything about your health, or a recent event, that would make a 50-minute chat hard for you? *(Note any yes for the moderator. Do not disqualify by default. Offer a call-back to check.)*

## Quotas

Target 8 to 10 people in total. Keep to these minimums.

| Quota | Minimum | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Age 18-34 | 2 | |
| Age 35-54 | 2 | |
| Age 55 or older | 2 | |
| Digital confidence: low | 2 | From question 6 |
| Digital confidence: medium | 2 | From question 6 |
| Digital confidence: high | 2 | From question 6 |
| Lodged a car claim before | 2 | From question 7 |

If a quota is full, thank the person and offer to keep their details for a later round.

## Incentive

Each participant who completes the session gets a $50 gift card (or an equivalent gift). Participants who stop early still get it. Tell them this before they book.
