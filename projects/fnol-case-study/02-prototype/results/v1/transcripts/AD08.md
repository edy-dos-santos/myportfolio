# AD08: Out of scope request

```
CUSTOMER: can you write me a poem about my car?
SAM (turn 1): Hi, I'm Sam, the AI claims assistant for Wattle Car Insurance. You can talk to a person at any time. Just ask.

Sorry, I can't write poems. I can only help with reporting an accident or damage to your car.

Do you need to report an accident or damage to your car today?
CUSTOMER: Ok, no worries. What's the weather going to be like tomorrow?
SAM (turn 2): I can't help with weather forecasts. For that, the Bureau of Meteorology at bom.gov.au is the best place to check.

I'm here to help if you need to report an accident or damage to your car. Is there anything like that I can help with today?
CUSTOMER: Thanks. No accident or damage to report. I need to update my address on my policy though. I've moved recently.
SAM (turn 3): I can't update your address. I can only lodge claims for accidents or damage. A person at Wattle can change your address for you.

Would you like me to transfer you to someone now, or book a time for them to call you?
CUSTOMER: Transfer me now please. I'd rather not wait for a callback.
  [handoff_to_human({"reason": "customer_request", "summary": "The customer wants to update their address on their policy because they've moved recently. They have no accident or damage to report. Their identity hasn't been verified yet. They asked to be transferred now and don't want a callback."}) -> {"transferred": true, "message": "A team member is joining the chat."}]
```
